# Books

## Free Online Books

- **[PostgreSQL Documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/)** — the ultimate reference. Free, comprehensive, well-written.
- **[The Art of PostgreSQL](https://theartofpostgresql.com/)** by Dimitri Fontaine — free PDF, excellent for SQL mastery.
- **[PostgreSQL Internals](https://www.postgresqlinternals.org/)** — free, deep dive into how PostgreSQL works internally.

## Paid Books (Worth Buying)

- **"PostgreSQL: Up and Running"** by Regina Obe and Leo Hsu — practical guide
- **"PostgreSQL High Performance"** by Gregory Smith — performance tuning
- **"Designing Data-Intensive Applications"** by Martin Kleppmann — not Postgres-specific but essential for understanding database systems
- **"SQL Antipatterns"** by Bill Karwin — common SQL mistakes and how to fix them
