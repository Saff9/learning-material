# 12 - Career & Job Prep

> **Phase:** 4 (Security & Career) · **Time:** ongoing · **Difficulty:** ⭐⭐

## What it is
Turning self-taught skills into a **job or freelance work**: a portfolio, resume, interview skills, and continuous learning.

## Why it matters
Skills alone don't get hired. You need **proof** (projects), **communication**, and **interview practice**. Self-taught devs are hired on demonstrated ability, not degrees.

## Core pillars — detailed

### Portfolio (most important)
- **3+ real projects** on GitHub, each with a README, screenshots, and a live demo if possible.
- Show **range**: one web app, one systems/CLI tool, one data project.
- Quality > quantity. One polished project beats five half-done.

### Resume
- One page; **outcomes** not duties ("built X used by Y users", not "responsible for X").
- Link GitHub + portfolio site.
- Tailor per role; skip irrelevant filler.

### Technical interviews
- **DSA round:** practice [[02 - Data Structures & Algorithms]] on LeetCode/NeetCode.
- **System design:** learn from the [[10 - Distributed Systems]] note + System Design Primer.
- **Behavioral:** STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
- **Communicate** your thinking out loud.

### Networking & open source
- Contribute to open source (docs/typos count).
- Share learnings on LinkedIn / a blog.
- Join communities (Discord, local meetups).

### Learning how to learn
- Tech changes; the *skill of learning* is permanent.
- Use the **Feynman method:** explain it simply or you don't know it yet.
