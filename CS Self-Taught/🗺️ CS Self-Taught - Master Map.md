# 🗺️ CS Self-Taught — Master Map

> A structured, self-paced **Computer Science** curriculum for self-taught developers.
> Compiled from public roadmaps: **TeachYourselfCS**, **roadmap.sh/computer-science**, **OSSU**, and **CS50**.

## 🎯 Who this is for
- Self-taught devs / bootcamp grads filling CS gaps.
- Career-changers who want fundamentals, not just frameworks.
- Anyone who wants to *understand* computing, not just use it.

## 🧭 How to use this vault
1. Start at **00 - Programming Fundamentals** and move **down in order**.
2. Each topic note has: *What it is · Why it matters · Detailed concepts · Free resources · Practice · Self-check · Progress*.
3. Tick the `Progress` boxes as you go; review [[CS Self-Taught Progress]] weekly.
4. **Build projects after every topic** — theory without practice doesn't stick.
5. Use the **Feynman method**: if you can't explain it simply, revisit it.

## 💡 Self-taught wisdom (from the research)
- **One language, one stack at a time.** Don't jump around.
- **Ship projects.** Evidence of building beats certificates.
- **Systems thinking:** learn trade-offs, not just code.
- **Feynman method:** explain it simply or you don't know it.
- **Daily practice**, even 1 hour. Consistency > intensity.
- **Avoid tutorial hell:** balance watching with building.
- **Ask for help.** Questions are good; quitting is the only failure.
- At 15–20 hrs/week, a job-ready foundation usually takes **~6–12 months**.
